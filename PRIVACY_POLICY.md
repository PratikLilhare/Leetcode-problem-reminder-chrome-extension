# Privacy Policy for LeetCode Problem Reminder & Tracker

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Thank you for using the LeetCode Problem Reminder & Tracker ("we", "our", or "the Extension"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Chrome Extension.

## 2. Information We Collect
**We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.** 

The Extension operates entirely locally on your device. It does not connect to any external servers, APIs, or third-party databases.

## 3. Local Storage and Permissions
To function properly, the Extension requires specific browser permissions:
* **Storage Permission:** We use the `chrome.storage.local` API to save your problem review schedules, problem URLs, and titles. This data is stored strictly on your local hard drive and is never transmitted off your device.
* **Host Permissions (`*://*.leetcode.com/*`):** We require access to LeetCode's domain strictly to inject our floating widget onto the page and read the URL of the current problem you are viewing for the autofill feature. 
* **Tabs/ActiveTab Permission:** Used locally to read the URL of your active tab solely for the purpose of autofilling the problem input field.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising services. 

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. Any updates will be reflected in the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and published on our Chrome Web Store listing or repository.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support tab on the Chrome Web Store listing.
